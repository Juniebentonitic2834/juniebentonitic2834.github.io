📥 What is NanoJev?

NanoJev is a compact, powerful tool that brings the core capabilities of Jev to your fingertips. Think of it as a miniature version of a sophisticated decision-making engine. It handles parallel decisions (handling multiple choices at once), works with dynamic candidates (adapting to changing options), and includes an end-to-end training pipeline (a complete process from data input to final results).

💻 System Requirements

📖 Quick Start Guide

Here's how to begin using NanoJev right after installation:

  1. Launch NanoJev: Double-click the NanoJev icon on your desktop or find it in your Start Menu.
  2. Choose Your Task: Pick from the main menu options — each one handles a different aspect of decision-making.
  3. Import Your Data: Click "Import" and select your file (CSV, Excel, or text files work best).
  4. Let It Work: Press "Run" and watch NanoJev process your data with lightning speed.
  5. View Results: Results appear instantly with beautiful charts and easy-to-read summaries.
